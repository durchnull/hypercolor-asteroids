While v9 was on the cabinet, David Friedrich changed the rules.

landed as “The book plays something while you read it” — 07 August 2026

The rules themselves were altered: tools/chronicle.sh now writes two more files nobody typed - docs/chronicle-song.js, the room a chapter is read in, and docs/chronicle.js, the book in the one shape the splash screen can read without fetching anything. Both are machine-written and neither is anybody's work, so tools/golden-check.sh has to know that before it starts asking who deleted what. tools/docs.sh dresses the same switch on the notes pages, because walking out of a chapter into the architecture should not be walking out of the building. And .claude/settings.json stops asking permission for this project's own tools, once per run, forever.

On this day David Friedrich invoked an override: GR6 - the soundtrack is one idea and thirteen hundred lines of it. A sequencer written in POSIX sh is verbose rather than complicated, and splitting it would invent an afternoon nobody worked.
